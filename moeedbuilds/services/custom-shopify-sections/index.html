FAQ

Custom section questions

Still unsure? Message me on WhatsApp and you will get a straight answer, usually the same day.

How is a custom section different from a page builder like PageFly or GemPages?

A page builder is an app that sits on top of your theme and loads its own code. A custom section is written directly into your theme in Liquid, so it renders like the rest of your store, needs no subscription, and keeps working if you ever uninstall an app.

Can I edit the section myself after it is built?

Yes, that is the point. Every section comes with theme editor settings for its text, images, colours and layout, plus blocks you can add, remove and reorder. You never need to open the code to change content.

Will it work with my theme?

Custom sections work with any Online Store 2.0 theme, including Dawn, Horizon, Prestige, Impulse and custom themes. I match your existing fonts, colours and spacing so the new section looks like it was always there.

How long does a custom section take?

A single section is usually a few days. A full set for a new homepage or landing page is typically seven to ten business days. You get the exact timeline with your quote.

What happens when my theme updates?

Custom sections live in your theme files, so they carry over when you duplicate or update in place. If you move to a completely new theme, the section file can be copied across and restyled rather than rebuilt from nothing.

How much does a custom section cost?

It depends on scope. A single section is a small fixed price job, while a full page of sections is quoted as a project. Send the design or an example and you will have a fixed quote within 24 hours. You can also see starting prices here.
