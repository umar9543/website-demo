FAQ

Liquid development questions

Still unsure? Message me on WhatsApp and you will get a straight answer, usually the same day.

What is Shopify Liquid?

Liquid is the templating language every Shopify theme is written in. It takes your store data, such as products, collections, prices and metafields, and turns it into the pages your customers see. Anything a theme does, it does through Liquid.

Can you work on my existing theme?

Yes. Most of my work is on existing themes, including Dawn, Horizon, Prestige, Impulse and fully custom ones. I work within your theme's structure so it stays maintainable and can still be updated.

Can custom Liquid replace an app I pay for?

Often, yes. Apps that add a single feature, like product badges, size charts, free shipping bars or simple bundles, can usually be rebuilt in Liquid. You pay once instead of monthly and lose a third party script. Complex apps with their own backend, such as subscriptions or reviews platforms, usually should stay apps.

Will my live store be affected while you work?

No. Everything is built on a duplicate of your theme. You review it on a preview link, and it only goes live when you approve it.

How do payments work?

Half upfront to start and half on completion, once you are happy with the result. You get a fixed quote within 24 hours of sending your brief, so there are no surprises.

Do you fix bugs and broken themes?

Yes. Layouts that broke after a theme update, app conflicts, mobile display problems and Liquid errors are all common jobs. Send the store URL and a description and I will tell you what is wrong.
