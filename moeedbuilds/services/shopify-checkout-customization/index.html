01

The old pages were replaced

Shopify set August 26, 2026 as the deadline for non-Plus stores to upgrade their Thank you and Order status pages. Any store that had not upgraded was switched over automatically, and code in Additional Scripts does not run on the new pages.

02

Checkout kept working, so nobody noticed

Customers still check out and orders still arrive. What stops is the reporting: conversion pixels, affiliate postbacks and post-purchase extras that lived in that box. Ad platforms simply start seeing fewer sales than you actually made.

03

Rebuilt the supported way

Tracking moves to web pixels and customer events, and on-page content moves to Thank you and Order status page blocks. Both are Shopify's supported foundation, so the next platform update does not wipe them out again.

Proven with a real order

FAQ

Checkout and tracking questions

Still unsure? Message me on WhatsApp and you will get a straight answer, usually the same day.

What changed on August 26, 2026?

That was Shopify's deadline for stores on non-Plus plans to upgrade their Thank you and Order status pages. Pages that had not been upgraded were switched to the new versions automatically, and customisations built with Additional Scripts or script tag apps do not run on them.

Is my checkout broken?

Almost certainly not. Customers can still buy and orders still come in. What usually breaks is tracking and anything shown on the Thank you page, which is why many stores have not noticed yet.

I am on Shopify Plus. Does this apply to me?

Plus stores had an earlier deadline, August 28, 2025, so if you are on Plus this change has already happened to your store. The same audit and rebuild applies if your tracking was never moved over.

Can I paste my old scripts somewhere else?

Not as they were. The new pages do not run arbitrary scripts. Tracking has to be rebuilt as web pixels, which run in a sandbox and listen to customer events, and on-page content has to be rebuilt as blocks.

How do you prove the tracking works?

With real test orders, checked in Google Tag Assistant, Meta Events Manager and each platform's own debugger. You see every event fire before sign-off, and I monitor for 48 hours after launch.

How long does it take?

It depends on how much was in your old checkout. A store with a few standard pixels is quick; one with custom affiliate postbacks and Thank you page extras takes longer. Send your store URL and you will get a scoped quote within 24 hours.
