Before We Start

What I'll need from you

Gathering this early is the single biggest thing that keeps a project on schedule. Don't worry if you don't have all of it yet.

  • Brand guidelines Colors, fonts, and your logo files. If you don't have a brand guide, just send what you have.
  • Design references Two or three sites you like the look of. This tells me more in 30 seconds than a page of description.
  • Your content Product images, copy, and anything that needs to appear on the pages we're building.
  • Shopify collaborator access I'll send instructions for this. Collaborator access means I never need your password.

Not ready with all of it? That's normal. We can start with what you have and fill the gaps as we go. I'll send a simple checklist after our call so nothing gets forgotten.

How I Work

What you're actually getting

Some things about my approach that are worth knowing upfront.

Custom Liquid, not page builders

No Shogun, PageFly, or GemPages. Page builder apps add JavaScript bloat and monthly fees. Everything I build is clean Liquid that loads at Shopify CDN speed and costs nothing to keep.

You can edit it yourself

Every section I build comes with theme editor settings. Text, images, colors, and ordering are yours to change without needing a developer for content updates.

Your live store stays safe

All work happens on a duplicate theme. Nothing goes live until you've seen it on a preview link and approved it.

Mobile is not an afterthought

Most of your traffic is on phones, so every section is built and tested for small screens first, then scaled up for tablet and desktop.

Money and Terms

How payment works

50% upfront, 50% on completion

You pay the second half only once you're satisfied with the work. That keeps the risk on my side, where it belongs.

Revisions included

How many depends on the plan we agree: one round on Starter, three on Professional, unlimited on Enterprise. It's written into the contract so there are no surprises.

On scope changes: if you want something added mid-project that wasn't in the original quote, I'll tell you the cost before doing it. You will never get an invoice for work you didn't approve.
